Rusoom privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Rusoom helps parents plan school fees and payment dates. It has no server and no accounts. We do not collect, receive or sell any of your data.

What stays on your phone

A child's nickname, school costs, payment dates and refund details are stored only on your phone. If your phone's own backup is on, Google or Apple may include this data in your personal backup. We cannot see it.

The school letter

Names you type for the letter are not saved. If you share the letter, it goes to whichever app you choose.

Reminders

Reminders are created on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store under their own privacy policies. We receive no card details.

Analytics and ads

Rusoom has no analytics and no ads. The stores give us overall figures such as installs and crash reports, under their own terms.

Children

Rusoom is for parents and guardians, and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete a child in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove the data from your phone.
